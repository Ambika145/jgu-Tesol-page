About the Programme

M.A. in TESOL (Teaching English to Speakers of Other Languages)

Master the Art of Teaching English Globally 

The online M.A. in TESOL equips you with globally relevant teaching skills in just one year. Learn second language acquisition, modern teaching methods, curriculum design, and assessment—so you can confidently create effective English learning experiences for diverse learners.

With a focus on digital pedagogy and AI-integrated learning, you gain practical exposure through live sessions and expert mentorship. Graduate as a skilled educator ready to teach, train, and lead across schools, universities, NGOs, and corporate learning environments worldwide.
